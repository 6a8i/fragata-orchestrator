# Support

:::tip
To run a self-diagnosis or open a secure support tunnel from your XOA, see [Troubleshooting](../getting-started/troubleshooting.md#still-stuck).
:::

Fragata runs in a controlled and tested environment thanks to XOA (the [Fragata virtual Appliance](https://vates.tech/deploy/)). **This is the only way to get pro support**. Any account with a registered XOA can open tickets from its personal space on [account.vates.tech](https://account.vates.tech).

Professional support is delivered through the [Vates VMS bundles](https://docs.vates.tech/pricing-licencing/vms-bundle-overview/), which cover your whole stack: XCP-ng, Fragata and the support itself. Higher tiers include faster ticket response times and cover more features: see [the plans and response times](https://vates.tech/pricing-and-support/).

## XOA Free support

With the free version of the Fragata Appliance (XOA free), **you can open private support tickets** and we will do our best to assist you. However, this support is limited and is not guaranteed in regards to response times or resolutions offered.

## Community support

While Fragata is free and Open Source software, supporting and developing it takes a lot of effort. If you are considering using Fragata in production, please subscribe to one of our [professional support plans](https://vates.tech/pricing-and-support/).

Read more in the [dedicated section for community support](community.md).
